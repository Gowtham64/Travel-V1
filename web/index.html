Privacy Policy & Data

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Information we use

We process account details you submit, such as your name and email address, along with trip inputs like origins, destinations, dates, selected vehicles and saved trips. Location is used only when you choose a current-location feature or grant the relevant device permission.

Why we use it

Trip information powers route planning, travel estimates, saved journeys and support. Limited technical data may be used to operate, secure and improve the service. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

  • You can decline device-location permission and enter places manually.
  • You can sign out and request help with account or data questions at any time.
  • Third-party map, authentication and payment providers may process data under their own policies when their services are used.
