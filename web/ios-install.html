Install Voyplan on your iPhone

Apple doesn't allow one-tap installs from a website like Android does — so you install it once from your computer using a free tool called Sideloadly. It takes about 5 minutes.

Download the app fileVoyplan.ipa · ~20 MB

Save this Voyplan.ipa file on your computer — you'll load it into Sideloadly in Step 4.

Good to know: This uses a free Apple ID, so the app stays active for 7 days. When it stops opening, just repeat Step 4 to refresh it (your data is kept). No payment, no jailbreak.

What you'll need

  • A Mac or Windows PC and a USB cable.
  • A free Apple ID (your normal one works; a spare Apple ID is fine too).
  • The Voyplan.ipa file from the button above.

Step-by-step

Follow these in order. The Win notes only apply to Windows PCs.

  1. Download the app file

    Tap “Download the app file” above and save Voyplan.ipa somewhere easy to find (e.g. your Downloads folder).

  2. Install Sideloadly on your computer

    Go to sideloadly.io, download Sideloadly for your system, and install it.

    Win Also install iTunes and iCloud from apple.com (the Apple website versions, not the Microsoft Store versions) — Sideloadly needs their drivers.

  3. Connect your iPhone

    Plug the iPhone into the computer with the cable. Unlock it, and if it asks, tap Trust This Computer and enter your passcode.

  4. Sign & install with Sideloadly

    Open Sideloadly. Drag Voyplan.ipa into the Sideloadly window (into the “IPA” box).

    Enter your Apple ID email in the Apple account field, then click Start. Enter your Apple ID password when asked. (If you use two-factor auth, you may need an app-specific password from appleid.apple.com.)

    Wait until Sideloadly shows “Done” — Voyplan now appears on your home screen (it may look greyed out until the next step).

  5. Trust the app on your iPhone

    On the iPhone open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management (older iOS: Profiles & Device Management).

    Tap your Apple ID / Developer App entry → tap Trust “…” → confirm Trust.

  6. Open Voyplan 🎉

    Launch Voyplan from the home screen. That's it — plan trips, budgets, bookings and your travel gallery, right on your iPhone.

Keeping it working

With a free Apple ID, the signature lasts 7 days. When Voyplan won't open, just repeat Step 4 (plug in, drag the same Voyplan.ipa, Start). Your saved trips and photos stay on the device.

Troubleshooting

Sideloadly can't find my iPhone

Make sure the phone is unlocked and you tapped Trust This Computer. On Windows, confirm iTunes (from apple.com) is installed and can see the phone. Try a different USB cable/port.

“Unable to install” / signing failed

Double-check the Apple ID email/password. If you have two-factor authentication, create an app-specific password at appleid.apple.com and use that in Sideloadly. A free Apple ID can have at most 3 sideloaded apps at once — remove old ones if needed.

The app icon is there but won't open

You missed Step 5 — go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and tap Trust on your Apple ID entry.

It stopped working after a week

That's the normal 7-day free-signing limit. Repeat Step 4 to re-sign it; nothing is lost.

Don't want to install anything? Open the web app in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen to use Voyplan full-screen like an app — free, instant, no expiry.
