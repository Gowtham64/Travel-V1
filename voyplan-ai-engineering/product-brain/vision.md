# VoyPlan — Product Vision & Strategy

## 1. Vision Statement
To become the world's most trustworthy and delightful road trip and travel companion—combining generative AI reasoning with deterministic spatial geometry and in-car connected vehicle telemetry.

## 2. Core Strategic Pillars
1. **Absolute Spatial Correctness**: AI never generates unrealistic hops, impossible timelines, or geographically disconnected detours.
2. **True Cross-Platform Parity**: The traveler experiences identical reliability on desktop web before the trip and on mobile/dashboard displays (Android Auto / CarPlay) during the drive.
3. **Continuous Autonomous Engineering**: The platform improves 24/7 through an autonomous AI engineering organization that investigates real telemetry, detects defects, debates architecture, and deploys high-assurance code.
