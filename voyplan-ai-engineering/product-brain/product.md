# VoyPlan — Product Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
VoyPlan is an AI-powered, cross-platform travel planning and real-time navigation ecosystem designed to eliminate uncertainty and friction in multi-day road trips, spiritual pilgrimages, vacations, and regional travel.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
- **Deterministic AI Itineraries**: Generate day-by-day sightseeing and travel itineraries with guaranteed spatial accuracy, route corridor adherence, and zero hallucinated distant cities.
- **Unified Multi-Platform Experience**: Seamless operation across Web SPA (`voyplan.in`), Android (with Android Auto vehicle bridge), and iOS (with Apple CarPlay voice integration).
- **Cost & Logistics Intelligence**: Integrated toll fare computation (FASTag), live fuel consumption forecasting, and vehicle database profiles.
- **Interactive Geospatial Navigation**: Native Mapbox GL + OSRM routing with real-time waypoint progression, live bearing, and turn-by-turn tracking.

## 3. Product Boundaries
VoyPlan treats the Website, Android app, iOS app, Backend API, Database, and Itinerary Engine as a single unified product. Any change on one platform requires verified consistency across the others.
